Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

System Doctor ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Access

System Doctor operates with a strict offline-first, privacy-respecting architecture. To provide device diagnostics, storage cleanup, and system monitoring:

  • Storage & Files: We inspect local storage directories and file sizes solely to help you identify large files and clean cached junk data. All scans occur locally on your device.
  • Device & App Telemetry: We monitor running process states, battery levels, RAM utilization, and installed application metadata locally to generate diagnostic reports.

2. Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect, upload, store, or share any personal data, file contents, or telemetry metrics to external servers or third-party entities. All diagnostics and optimizations are processed entirely on-device.

3. Permissions

The application requests necessary Android permissions (such as package querying or storage access) exclusively to perform local device doctor functions as requested by you.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate external analytics, telemetry trackers, or advertising SDKs.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@systemdoctor.app.
